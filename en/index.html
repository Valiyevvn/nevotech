    Frequently asked questions

    If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp.

    Can I order services separately?

    Yes. Each module works independently. Anything you add later connects to your existing system.

    How does the free demo work?

    We build a sample system for your business and show it live. No payment and no obligation: if you don’t like it, you owe nothing.

    How long does a project take?

    A simple website takes 1–2 weeks; ERP and management systems take 1–3 months depending on scope.

    How is the price set?

    The price depends on the modules you choose and the scope of features. We send an exact quote after the introduction.

    Will it work with the software we use now?

    In most cases, yes. We look at integration options with your current software together.

    Is our data protected?

    Yes. Data is kept on secured servers with regular backups. You decide what each employee can see.

    Do you offer support after handover?

    Yes. We provide technical support after handover and update the system when needed.
